# Practical Checklist Section Design

## Introduction to Checklist Section
The Practical Checklist section will be the most robust part of the guide, with distinct visual styling to make it highly actionable and usable. Each category will have its own dedicated page with consistent formatting.

## Global Section Layout
- Section title "Practical Checklist" in Deep Blue (#052d3f), 28pt
- Brief introduction paragraph from guide content
- Five categories, each with distinct icons:
  1. Everyday Essentials (House icon)
  2. Health & Wellness Routines (HeartPlus icon)
  3. Financial Flow & Future Care (Wallet icon)
  4. Home & Comfort (Armchair icon)
  5. Family & Community Connection (MessageCircle icon)

## Category Layout Structure
```
+-------------------------------------+
|                                     |
```

## Visual Hierarchy
- Category icon and title: Most prominent element
- "What to Do:" subtitle: Secondary heading
- Checklist items: Clear, action-oriented content
- Tip box: Supportive advice that stands out visually

## Icon Style and Size
- Icon size: Approximately 0.75" square
- Style: Outline style from Lucide icons
- Color: Deep Blue (#052d3f)
- Positioned left of the category title

## Checklist Item Styling
- Bullet style: Warm Brown (#a57c52) check mark
- Indentation: 0.25" from left margin after check mark
- Line spacing: 1.5 (same as body text)
- Item spacing: 12pt between items
