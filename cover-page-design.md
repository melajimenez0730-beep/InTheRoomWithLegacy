## Design Elements

### Background
- Full-page image: Family-legacy portrait showing multiple generations
- Deep Blue overlay (#052d3f) at 80% opacity to provide contrast for text
- The overlay gradient should be darker at the bottom to ensure text readability

### Logo Placement
- "In the Room with Legacy™" logo in white
- Positioned at the top quarter of the page
- Size: approximately 3 inches wide
- Logo used: `/mnt/tmp-projects/project-9616/media/1_nw_659b730b.png` (white version)

### Typography
- Title: "PARENTING A PARENT"
  - Font: Serif font (Garamond or similar)
  - Size: 48pt
  - Color: White (#ffffff)
  - Weight: Bold
  - Positioning: Left-aligned, centered vertically on the page

- Subtitle: "A Guide for Adult Children"
  - Font: Same serif font as title
  - Size: 24pt
  - Color: White (#ffffff)
  - Weight: Regular
  - Positioning: Left-aligned, below the title with appropriate spacing

### Additional Design Notes
- Maintain 0.75" margins on all sides
- The title and subtitle should stand out clearly against the background
- The overall aesthetic should be professional, warm, and dignified
- The overlay should be dark enough to provide contrast but not completely obscure the emotional impact of the background image
