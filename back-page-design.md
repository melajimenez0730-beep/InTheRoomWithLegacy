# Back Page/CTA Page Design

## Overall Design
The back page will serve as a powerful call-to-action (CTA) page that encourages further engagement with the organization's services and resources. The design will use high contrast with a Deep Blue background and white text for maximum impact and visibility.

## Design Elements

### Background
- Solid Deep Blue (#052d3f) background for entire page
- Subtle tree branch motif in slightly lighter blue in corners (echoing the tree in the logo)

### Typography
- Thank You Heading:
  - Font: Serif font (Garamond or similar)
  - Size: 28pt
  - Color: White (#ffffff)
  - Weight: Bold
  - Alignment: Left-aligned

- Subheading:
  - Font: Serif font (Garamond or similar)
  - Size: 18pt
  - Color: White (#ffffff)
  - Weight: Regular
  - Alignment: Left-aligned
  - Spacing: 24pt below thank you heading, 24pt above first CTA button

### CTA Buttons
- Three distinct buttons with identical styling
- Style:
  - Background: None/transparent
  - Border: 2pt solid White (#ffffff)
  - Border radius: 5pt
  - Padding: 16pt
  - Margin: 16pt between buttons
  - Width: 4" (fixed width)
  - Height: Auto (expands based on content)
- Typography:
  - Font: Sans-serif font (Open Sans or similar)
  - Size: 14pt
  - Color: White (#ffffff)
  - Weight: Bold
  - Alignment: Centered within button
  - Case: All caps for high visibility
- Hover effect (for digital version):
  - Background: White (#ffffff)
  - Text color: Deep Blue (#052d3f)

### Logo and Contact Information
- Logo:
  - "In the Room with Legacy™" logo (white version)
  - Width: 2.5"
  - Position: Centered, 1.5" from bottom of page
- Contact Information:
  - Font: Sans-serif font (Open Sans or similar)
  - Size: 10pt
  - Color: White (#ffffff)
  - Weight: Regular
  - Alignment: Centered
  - Spacing: 12pt below logo

### Visual Hierarchy
1. Thank you heading (largest text)
2. CTA buttons (high contrast, prominent)
3. Subheading (medium emphasis)
4. Logo (brand reinforcement)
5. Contact information (smallest text)

### Additional Design Notes
- The buttons should have sufficient spacing to prevent accidental mispresses in digital versions
- For print versions, consider adding a QR code that links to the main website alongside contact information
- The design balances white space with content for a clean, professional appearance
- All text should have sufficient contrast against the Deep Blue background for readability
- The page should feel like a natural conclusion to the guide while prompting next steps
